Valentine's Day Gift Giving Data Visualization Website

This website presents a Valentine’s Day data visualization based on a 2024 National Retail Foundation (NRF) survey of 7,728 U.S. adults. It focuses on the three most popular and culturally significant gift categories—candy, flowers, and jewelry—to highlight consumer behavior and the emotional meaning behind gift-giving. The goal was to create a creative yet accessible representation of the data.

Website Created With: Illustrator, Canva

Renewable Energy Data Visualization Website

I created a four-page website to present interactive data visualizations on renewable energy.The website features an introductory page that sets the context, followed by three pages showcasing different visualizations (Time Series, Chloropleth, and Scatterplot). Each visualization allows users to interact with the data, providing an in-depth look at renewable energy trends, usage, and statistics.
